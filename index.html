Testing

Verified SGS, BV, Intertek, REACH, CPSC, Prop 65, or nickel-release testing should be paid to the lab directly by the buyer.

SX Jewelry requests factory material declarations and third-party test reports on the buyer's behalf. SX Jewelry does not certify compliance.
